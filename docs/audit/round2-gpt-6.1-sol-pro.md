## Verdict — C; publishable as-is: no

The architecture remains sensible, and the previous audit removed meaningful risks. However, the unattended-bootstrap fix still does not enforce its central promise: Caddy starts before initialization is verified, failures are treated as success, and an interrupted first run can permanently skip initialization. Re-running also overwrites VPN-only policy with an open configuration. These are release blockers for an installer advertised as hardened and safe to rerun. This is a static review of the supplied bundle, not a live-server verification; `egress.json` and the raw audit reports were not supplied, so their contents cannot be validated.
